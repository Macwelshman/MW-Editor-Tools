Selecting an unrelated prefab, leaving/loading the editor, explicit history clearing or an unrelated committed edit still invalidates it. Undo operates on the captured source and exact snapshots, never on the newly selected prefab. Root/component/reference/settings/source-list guards and foliage completion checks remain unchanged.

The shared replacement slot no longer requires an enabled inspector for Undo. Save, dialog, recovery availability and busy-operation gates remain. Deferred replacement Ctrl+Z pins the action identity and its context lease rather than the transient inspector asset; the original selection gate remains for other recovery actions.
